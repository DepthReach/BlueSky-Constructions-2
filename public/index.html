About Blue Sky

Owner-led, built around estate homes.

Blue Sky Water Proofing & Construction is run by Lymon Chakanyuka. The company handles waterproofing, roofing, renovations, paving, painting and building for homes in and around Hartbeespoort.

The aim is straightforward: look after the property and make it easy to reach the person responsible. Call or WhatsApp 076 613 3663 and you will speak to the business directly.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Which areas do you work in?

Hartbeespoort and its luxury estates, including Pecanwood, The Islands, Westlake and Xanadu. We also serve Rustenburg and Mbombela.

What waterproofing do you do?

Roof sealing, torch-on membrane, damp proofing and leak repairs.

Do you do building work as well?

Yes. We build houses, renovate and extend existing homes, and manage the project, as well as paving, painting and roofing.

How do I get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp, call 076 613 3663 or use the quote form on this page. Tell us the type of job and where it is, and send photos if you have them.

Can you assess my property first?

An assessment is part of the process before a quotation. Get in touch and we will arrange the next step.

Are you licensed and insured?

Blue Sky operates as a licensed and insured contractor. Ask us for documents when you request your quote.
